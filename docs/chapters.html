Instructions: Before you read...

Each white box represents one page of the manhwa. We use the following styles to show how text appears in the original:

  • Speech Bubblecontain dialogue spoken aloud. We display the dialogue in regular text and show the speaker’s name in red, as shown below:

    Sung Jin-Woo: Haah

  • Thought Bubblescontain a character’s private thoughts. We display them in italics, as shown below:

    Sung Jin-Woo: MY NAME IS SUNG JIN-WOO.

  • Information caption: identify details such as the location or time. We display them in bold., as shown below:

    SEOUL, SOUTH KOREA

  • Narrative caption: tell part of the story or describe what is happening. They can come from a narrator or from a character, such as Sung Jin-Woo. We display them in bold and include the character’s name when the narration is theirs, as shown below:

    Sung Jin-Woo: IF YOU DISREGARD MY SLIGHTLY ABNORMAL STRENGTH AND REGENERATION,

  • System captions: display messages from the System that helps Sung Jin-Woo level up. We give these messages a dark background., as shown below:

    ALARM

    [WELCOME, PLAYER.]
